⚖️ Honest Limitations Statement

  • Historical Sonification Only: This project sonifies historical, long-term observational climate records. It does not detect, predict, or warn about localized hazards or emergencies in real time.
  • Coarse Regional Resolution: Regional layers (including the Himalayan track) represent coarse gridded climate belts (25°–35°N, 75°–95°E) and cannot verify site-specific conditions at a single slope or glacier tongue.
  • Published Historical Context: Any glacier or disaster information (such as the Langtang Lirung 2026 reference) is historical context from published scientific sources (AntarcticGlaciers.org, World Weather Attribution), not something verified independently by our sonification script.
  • Data Fidelity: Numerical values are decoded verbatim from NASA's published open datasets (GISTEMP v4) and are only as precise as those scientific sources allow.
  • Extrapolation Disclaimer: The optional projection toggle in Track 1 is a simple linear regression extrapolation of the 1970–2024 warming slope, NOT a dynamic coupled general circulation climate model.
  • Assistive Technology Testing: Built with semantic HTML5, WCAG AAA contrast ratios, visible 3px focus rings, ARIA live regions, and screen-reader synchronized ducking.
